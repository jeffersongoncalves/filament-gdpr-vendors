# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

## 2.1.0 - 2026-11-17

19 languages: Arabic, Azerbaijani, Brazilian Portuguese, Chinese (Simplified), Dutch, English, French, German, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Persian, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Turkish, Ukrainian and Uzbek. GDPR-specific texts (articles, lawful bases, transfer mechanisms, deadlines) are translated with each language's official GDPR terms.

## 2.0.0 - 2026-11-17

First release: laravel-gdpr-vendors in your Filament panel. Vendors with role, risk tier, the Art. 28(3) processor contract checklist, a Chapter V transfer mechanism for vendors outside the EEA, an issues column and the review, end relationship and data returned actions. English.
